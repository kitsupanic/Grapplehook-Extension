This extension needs the Grapplehook app

This extension hands videos to the Grapplehook desktop app, version 0.3.0 or later. The app does the download.

If you press Send and nothing happens, or your browser shows an error, the app is probably missing or too old.

Get the latest Grapplehook

After you install it, press Send again. Your browser may ask once whether to open Grapplehook.
